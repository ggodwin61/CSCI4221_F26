## Scrum process

Team members own the product and voluntarily select backlog tasks. Weekly Scrum
meetings review completed work, blockers, and the next PBIs. Each course project
is a sprint; this course has four or five sprints with a typical 2–3 week velocity
window. The potentially shippable increment for Sprint 4 is the architecture
submission. The assignment separately requests three meetings within its 10-day
period. Only two are documented here. The announcement date and calendar due
date were not supplied; the stated deadline is 10 days after announcement.

Section 1 addresses architecture templates, cloud software, Docker, containers,
daemons, images, and database persistence. Section 2 covers major components,
sub-components, file responsibilities, proposed ownership, and the sprint backlog.
These are architecture discussion notes, not claims that every topic was covered
at the historical meetings.
