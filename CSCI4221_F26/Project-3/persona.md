# Campus Map Persona

**Name:** Garret Godwin  
**Group number:** Group 1

## Dr. Phillip Journey New Professor

### Personalization

Dr. Phillip Journey is a 46-year-old man who recently moved to the area to join ASU. He is still learning the campus layout and the locations of classrooms inside its buildings.

### Job Related Information

Dr. Journey is a newly hired cybersecurity professor. His work involves teaching classes and attending faculty meetings in different campus buildings. He needs to identify the correct building, floor, and classroom before teaching.

### Education and Technical Experience

He recently earned a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Georgia Tech and holds a master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Miami. He is familiar with cybersecurity, mobile applications, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and several programming languages. He is comfortable with smartphones and digital maps, but his technical experience does not give him knowledge of the campus layout.

### Relevance to the Campus Map

Dr. Journey wants to find his assigned classrooms and arrive ready to teach on time. His schedule may list a building abbreviation and room number without showing the floor or room location. He would use Classroom Search to identify the correct classroom, view its building and floor, and see its position on an available indoor floor map.
