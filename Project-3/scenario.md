# Campus Map Scenario

**Name:** Garret Godwin  
**Group number:** Group 1

## Finding an Assigned Classroom

**Overall objective:** Dr. Phillip Journey wants to locate his assigned classroom inside an unfamiliar building and arrive before his class begins.

**Persona involved:** [Dr. Phillip Journey](persona.md), a new ASU cybersecurity professor who is comfortable with technology but unfamiliar with the campus and its classroom layouts.

### Scenario Narrative

During his first week at ASU, Dr. Journey checks his teaching schedule twenty minutes before his next class. The schedule lists a building abbreviation and a classroom number, but it does not explain which floor the classroom is on or where it is located inside the building. Knowing the building alone would not help him find the correct room in time to prepare for his students.

Dr. Journey opens the campus map app on his smartphone and searches for the classroom number, using the building abbreviation to distinguish it from rooms with the same number elsewhere on campus. He selects the result matching his schedule. The app displays the building's full name, the classroom number, and the floor. An indoor floor map is available for this classroom, so it also shows the classroom's position relative to the floor's corridors and stairs.

Dr. Journey goes to the identified building and floor. He checks the classroom's highlighted location on the floor map, follows the corridor, and confirms the room number at the door. He arrives before class begins and has time to prepare. Classroom Search has helped him locate the room inside the building rather than stopping at the building's location.

**Activities:** Check the teaching schedule, search for a classroom, narrow the results by building, select the matching room, view its floor and indoor location, and go to the classroom.

**Problem:** The existing schedule identifies a classroom but does not show its floor or position within the building, leaving a new professor uncertain about where to go after entering.

**Proposed solution:** Classroom Search connects the scheduled room number and building to a classroom record with floor information and, where available, a highlighted indoor map location.
